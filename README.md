# Superstore-sales-analysis
Sales analysis of Sample Superstore dataset using |Excel pivot tables
Project 1: Sales Analysis — Sample Superstore Dataset
Objective: Analyze regional sales performance, category profitability, and the impact of discounting to identify business insights and recommendations.

# Q1: Which region sells the most?
Finding: West generated $725,458 in sales — the highest of all regions, representing 32% of total company revenue ($2,297,201). East followed closely at $678,781, while South trailed significantly at $391,722, roughly 46% lower than West.
Recommendation: Investigate what's driving West's strong performance (customer demand, sales team size, marketing spend) and assess whether similar tactics could lift South's numbers.

# Q2: Which category is most profitable?
Finding: Technology is the most profitable category ($145,455 profit, 17.4% margin), followed closely by Office Supplies ($122,491 profit, 17.0% margin). Furniture generated the second-highest sales ($741,999) but produced only $18,451 in profit — a margin of just 2.5%, far below the other categories.
Recommendation: Investigate why Furniture's margin is so low, likely high discounting or production/shipping costs. Consider reducing discount depth on Furniture or reviewing supplier costs.

# Q3: Does discount level hurt profit?
Finding: Yes — there's a clear negative relationship between discount depth and profit. Orders with no discount or a 10% discount average $67–$96 profit. Once discounts reach 30% or higher, average profit turns negative, dropping as low as -$310 at the 50% discount level.
Recommendation: Cap discounts at 20% wherever possible. Discounts of 30%+ should be reviewed, as they're likely eroding profit rather than driving profitable volume.

# Overall Conclusion: While West region and Technology category drive the strongest revenue and profit, Furniture's low margin and the negative impact of heavy discounting represent the biggest opportunities for margin improvement across the business.
